reWord Game is a free browser puzzle about sentences. This guide explains every part of the game so you can start playing with confidence.

The goal

Each level shows a sentence with its words in the wrong order. Your goal is to arrange the words so the sentence makes sense. When every word sits in its correct place, the level completes and the next level unlocks.

How to move words

Drag a word left or right to swap it with its neighbour. Words with a dotted underline can move. Words without one are locked, which means they stay put and act as anchors for the rest of the sentence.

Levels that grow with you

The first levels are gentle. One of them contains only three tiles, "Word", "re" and "Game", and asks you to build the game's own name. Later levels add more tiles, multi word tiles, locked words and full sentences such as "The quick fox jumps over the lazy dog." Each step adds a little more to think about, so the difficulty rises gradually.

Moves and time

Depending on the level, the game shows either a Moves counter or a clock. A Moves counter means you have a set number of swaps, so a little planning pays off. A clock simply records how long you took, so you can try again to beat your own time.

The Hint button

When you want a nudge, press Hint. The game highlights a word that is out of place for a moment, so you can see where to focus next. Hints are a helpful way to keep moving and to learn the pattern behind a sentence.

Saved progress

The game remembers the levels you have unlocked, so you can leave and return later. The play page continues from the next level waiting for you.

Play on any device

reWord Game runs in a modern web browser on desktop, tablet and phone. There is nothing to install and no signup. Drag with a mouse or with your finger.

Why sentences make good puzzles

Every sentence has a natural rhythm. Subjects lead into verbs, articles lead into nouns, and capital letters and full stops mark the edges. reWord turns that rhythm into a game: you use what you already know about language to spot the order, and each correct swap feels like a small discovery. Because the rules are simple, you can spend your attention on the fun part, which is working out the sentence.

A first level to try

Begin with the opening level. You will see three tiles, "Word", "re" and "Game". Drag "re" to the front and the title of the game appears. It is a quick way to feel how swapping works before the sentences become longer.

Share your level

The Share button on each page uses your device's share sheet when it is available, and the Copy link button places the page address on your clipboard. Send a tricky level to a friend and see who solves it first.

Quick start

  1. Open the play page and choose level one.
  2. Drag a word to swap it with its neighbour.
  3. Notice which words are locked and plan around them.
  4. Use Hint whenever you want a nudge.
  5. Finish the level and move on to the next.

You can learn the whole game in a minute and keep enjoying it for much longer. Come and give it a try.
